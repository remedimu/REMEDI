About REMEDI

Who We Are

A student-led research initiative spanning three Jordanian medical universities — Mutah University, the University of Jordan, and Ibn Sina University — founded to connect medical students with meaningful research opportunities.

Our Mission

REMEDI exists to cultivate a research culture among medical students. We believe that early exposure to research methodology, collaborative interest groups, and open mentorship shapes better physicians and advances healthcare in Jordan.

Our Goals

  • ✓Provide open-access courses that demystify research for medical students.
  • ✓Support interest groups with mentorship, resources, and project infrastructure.
  • ✓Promote student-led publication and presentation at regional conferences.
  • ✓Bridge clinical practice and research through faculty collaboration.
